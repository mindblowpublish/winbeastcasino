02Data processed through the App

The App uses OneSignal and Firebase Cloud Messaging to manage and deliver push notifications. Depending on your device, SDK version, and enabled settings, these services may process:

  • Notification identifiers: push tokens, subscription identifiers, and Firebase installation IDs used to route messages to an app installation.
  • Device and connection information: device model, operating system, language, app identifier, network carrier, and IP address. An IP address may also indicate an approximate location.
  • Subscription and activity information: notification permission status, subscription status, app session timestamps, session counts and duration, and notification delivery or interaction events where available.

Some technical data may be processed when the messaging SDKs initialize, even if notifications are disabled. Turning off notifications stops their display but does not necessarily delete existing identifiers or stop all SDK processing.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to provide.
